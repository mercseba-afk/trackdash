## What this already tells us

The pilot is beginning to separate three different market properties that TrackDash must never collapse into one score:

1. **Ticket value:** 18012 currently stands far above the rest of this P1 sample.
2. **Observed repeat-sale depth:** 18031 is the clearest example of a lower-priced Release with materially more clean observations.
3. **Tight collector cluster:** 18034 and 18038 show relatively narrow candidate price bands, but their sample sizes and current-source coverage are still too small to call a definitive public Market Value.

The next publication gate is cross-market confirmation. eBay sold evidence and/or another sufficiently current independent sold source should be added before treating these research medians as public valuation anchors. Asking prices remain a separate evidence class and cannot substitute for sold comps.
