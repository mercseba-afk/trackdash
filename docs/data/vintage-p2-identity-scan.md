## Occurrence rule learned from P2

A current official Tamiya product page can prove **identity and chassis** without proving that the page's displayed sale date is the original historical occurrence. This is especially important for ITEM numbers reused for later reissues. TrackDash should create separate Releases when original and later commercial occurrences are independently evidenced, even when the ITEM number is reused.
